You are writing example sentences for Hindi vocabulary flashcards.

The learner is a first-generation Indian-American, 16 or older. They understand a fair amount of
spoken Hindi from home/family but are building up reading vocabulary. They do NOT know advanced,
literary, or newspaper-register Hindi.

I will give you a Hindi word (in Devanagari) and its English meaning, for your reference.

## Your task

Write ONE short, natural sentence in Devanagari that uses the word in a simple, everyday context
a teenager or young adult would plausibly encounter — the kind of sentence you'd hear at home, at
a family gathering, texting a cousin, or talking about school/friends/daily life.

## Rules

1. EVERY OTHER WORD IN THE SENTENCE SHOULD ALREADY BE FAMILIAR OR EASY. Besides the target word
   itself, only use vocabulary that is either extremely common/basic Hindi (words a heritage
   speaker raised in the US would already know from home — family terms, food, everyday verbs
   like जाना/करना/होना/खाना/देखना, numbers, common objects) or so frequent that it's worth
   learning anyway. Do not reach for a literary, formal, or obscure word anywhere else in the
   sentence — the target word should be the only "new" thing in it.

2. KEEP IT SHORT. One clause or two short clauses joined simply (e.g. with और, लेकिन, क्योंकि,
   अगर). No long or nested sentences.

3. MAKE THE CONTEXT DO THE WORK. The surrounding words and situation should make the target
   word's meaning inferable, so the sentence itself is a second chance (beyond the images) to
   understand the word without needing the English translation.

4. GROUNDED, ORDINARY SITUATIONS ONLY. Prefer everyday scenes: family, food, school, friends,
   home, weather, weekend plans — not abstract, poetic, or news-style sentences.

5. WRAP THE TARGET WORD (in whatever inflected form it takes in your sentence) in `<b>` and
   `</b>` tags so it stands out on the card.

6. Devanagari only. No English translation, no transliteration, no extra commentary.

## Output format — STRICT

Reply with ONLY the sentence, nothing else — no label, no translation, no quotation marks around
it.

## Example

Hindi word: थका
English meaning: tired
Good output: आज स्कूल के बाद मैं बहुत <b>थका</b> हुआ हूँ।
