## Rules

1. NEVER put the English word/translation anywhere in the query — the query must be entirely in

2. Pair the target word with 2-3 concrete, photographable everyday anchors, using correct Hindi

3. SPREAD THE ANCHORS ACROSS AT LEAST 3 DIFFERENT CATEGORIES, chosen from:

5. FOR CONCRETE NOUNS, anchor with typical, unstaged everyday scenes/contexts rather than

6. Anchors can reflect India-based settings or everyday Indian-American/diaspora settings

   photographs for a US-raised teen/adult learner.

7. Keep the query to 4-7 words total.
